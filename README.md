# Hastanem
Hastanem is an indoor navigation project designed to help patients find their way around hospitals, with a particular focus on older adults who may find large, unfamiliar buildings difficult to navigate.

The Problem

GPS signals are unreliable indoors, making navigation through complex hospital buildings challenging. Hastanem explores an accessible alternative using Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) technology.

How It Works

BLE Beacons: Bluetooth Low Energy beacons provide signals that can be detected indoors.

ESP32-C3: Used in the hardware setup for the project.

RSSI-Based Localization: Received Signal Strength Indicator (RSSI) measurements are used to estimate the user's proximity to nearby beacons and help determine their approximate indoor location.

Navigation: The estimated location supports the goal of guiding users through the hospital.

My Contribution

I worked on the hardware side of the project, focusing on the ESP32-C3 and BLE beacon setup that supports the indoor localization system.

Our Goal

We aim to make navigating hospitals less confusing and help patients move around more independently.
